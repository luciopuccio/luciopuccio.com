HOW TO PLAY

  1. The screen starts at 3.
  2. Tap the next digit of π, then the next.
  3. One wrong digit ends the run. You see the right one, then start again from 3.
  4. Your longest run is your hi-score.

Tip: learn π in groups of five. At digit 762 you meet the Feynman point, six 9s in a row.
